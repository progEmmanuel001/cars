The History of Nissan GT-R

The Nissan GT-R heritage began in the late 1960s with the Skyline GT-R, quickly gaining a reputation on the racetrack. By the 1990s, the R32, R33, and R34 generations cemented the GT-R name as the ultimate Japanese performance car. The R35, launched in 2007, redefined what a sports car could be, blending power, technology, and daily usability. Today, the GT-R continues to be an icon for enthusiasts worldwide.
